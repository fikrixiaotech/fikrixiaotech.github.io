  1. Number of token

    2. The smaller the supply, the rarer the token, giving it a higher chance to become expensive

  2. Marcet Capitalization

    3. The larger the market cap, the higher the average price of the held tokens

  3. Market Prediction

Crypto Movements

  1. Bearish

    2. Market price is going down, shwon by red candle, inspired by bear attack

  2. Bullish

    3. Market price is going up, shown by green candle, inspried by bull attack

  3. Dip

    4. Price is down 1%-5%

  4. Pullback

    5. Price is down 5%-10%

  5. Correction

    6. Price is down 10%-20%

  6. Crash

    7. Price is down 20% or more

  7. To the Moon

    8. Market is experiencing an extreme upward trend

What Should Being Concern in Crypto

  1. Dont Buy a Green Candle

    2. Don't buy when the price is high, wait and see in another day and buy the dip

  2. Dont Catch a Fallen Knife

    3. Don't buy a dumping coin

  3. Buy Through a Limit Order

    4. Set a limit, don't buy with a market order

  4. Buy in the morning

    5. 08.00 WIB-10.00 WIB (or 01.00-03.00 UTC) is the dip time, right after the market open

    Opposite with 20.00-22.00 WIB (or 13.00-15.00 UTC or 05.00-07.00 PST) when market is going crazy

  5. Set an alarm

    6. to alert when it is time to sell or buy, without having to check constantly

  6. Current price shows the last transaction

    7. The price shown right now is determined by the last transaction, not the ask or bid price
